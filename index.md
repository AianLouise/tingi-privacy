# Tingi Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Tingi is a point-of-sale app for sari-sari stores, made by Aian Louise A. Alfaro. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in Tingi.

## The short version

Tingi does not collect, send, or sell your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

## What Tingi stores

To run your store, Tingi saves the following on your device only:

- Store details: store name, address, and contact number
- Products, prices, categories, stock levels, and product photos you add
- Sales, receipts, and cash shift records
- Customers you add, their contact numbers, and their utang (credit) balances and payments
- Suppliers you add, deliveries, and payables
- An activity log of actions taken in the app

This data is saved in a database inside the app's private storage. Tingi has no servers, and the app does not connect to the internet.

## When data leaves your phone

Data leaves your phone only when you choose to share it:

- **Receipts and reports.** When you tap Share or Export, Tingi creates a PDF, CSV, or Excel file and opens your phone's share menu. You choose where it goes, for example Messenger, email, or Google Drive. The app you share to handles the file under its own privacy policy.
- **Backups.** When you back up your database, you choose where to save or send the file. The backup file contains all your Tingi data, including customer details and product photos, and is not password-protected, so keep it somewhere private. When you restore, you choose the file to load.

Tingi does not upload anything on its own.

## Camera and photos

Tingi asks for camera access to scan product barcodes and to take product photos, and for photo library access to choose product photos.

- Barcode scans are read on your device and are not saved.
- Product photos you take or choose are saved in the app's private storage on your phone and are included in backups.

Nothing from the camera or photo library is sent anywhere. Tingi does not use the microphone.

## Your control over your data

- You can edit or delete products, customers, and suppliers at any time.
- **Settings → Reset All Data** permanently deletes everything Tingi has stored.
- Uninstalling Tingi deletes all of its data from your phone. Keep a backup first if you want to restore it later.

Because your data never reaches us, we cannot view, recover, or delete it for you. Make regular backups to protect your records.

## Children

Tingi is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.
